OUR PURPOSE

Money clarity, made personal.

My Finance Manager is a simple personal finance tool designed to help you understand your money, build better habits, and make everyday decisions with more confidence.

Simple by design

Add income and expenses, organise transactions, review accounts and keep an eye on budgets in one clear dashboard.

Your device, your data
